About Madraset Bozoor

The ideal environment to teach your child the Quran and Arabic, in a modern and enjoyable way

Our method

Understanding first, then memorization

We don't start with memorization; we start with the heart. When children understand what they recite, the words stay with them forever.

Why was this surah revealed?

We begin with the story of the surah and the reason it was revealed.

Reflecting on its meanings

Together we reflect on the meanings of the surah.

Understanding every verse

We explain each verse so your child understands what they recite.

Then memorization

We memorize it once its meanings have taken root in the heart.
